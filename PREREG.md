# CBIO008T "Deep Learning Pipeline for EZH2 Drug Discovery" - preregistration (before any model scored)
Data: PubChem PUG-REST concise bioactivity for gene EZH2 (GeneID 2146), confirmatory IC50 rows with CID and value, assays from US patents (USPTO-derived records), excluding assays whose name mentions EED or a cell line/proliferation. SMILES from PubChem. Hashes in data_manifest. Compound label = median pIC50 over its measurements.
Known problems stated up front: patent data mixes assay formats (LC-MS, AlphaScreen, ELISA, SPA, PRC2 vs EZH2 complexes), and many compounds come as close analogue series from one patent, so random splits will be optimistic.
Models (fixed hyperparameters, seed 0): Ridge(alpha=10), RandomForest(300 trees, max_features=0.33), MLP (sklearn, 512-128, early stopping). Features: Morgan radius 2, 2048 bits unless stated.
D1 curation counts. D2 random 5-fold CV (compound level). D3 leave-patent-out (GroupKFold 5 on first listed patent). Gate: best model LPO Spearman >=0.50. D4 model comparison on D2 and D3; gate for 'deep learning helps': MLP beats RF LPO Spearman by >=0.03.
D5 applicability domain: LPO test compounds binned by max Tanimoto to training (>=0.7, 0.5-0.7, <0.5); Spearman per bin. D6 potent (pIC50>=7) classification in LPO: AUROC and fraction of true potent in the top 10% predicted. D7 fingerprint ablation (RF, LPO): Morgan2 bits, Morgan3 counts, MACCS, RDKit topological. D8 inter-assay noise floor: for compounds measured in >=2 assays, Spearman and MAE of pIC50 between assays; compare to model RMSE. D9 known inhibitors (tazemetostat, GSK126, EPZ005687, CPI-1205 by PubChem name lookup): if present, LPO prediction vs measured. D10 scaffold split (Bemis-Murcko, GroupKFold 5) vs LPO.
No hyperparameter tuning on test folds. Honest negatives reported.
